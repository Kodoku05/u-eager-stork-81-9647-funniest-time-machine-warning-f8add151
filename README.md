Write the funniest one-line warning label for a time machine in 120 characters or fewer. An AI judge compares labels two at a time and picks the funnier one.

The entry: "WARNING: Do not kill your grandfather. You will be fined for the paperwork, and you will not exist to pay it." (109 characters)

The joke turns the grandfather paradox into a bureaucracy problem: erasing your own existence still leaves a fine for the paperwork — and the surprise is saved for the last few words, where the reader learns there will be nobody left to pay it.
